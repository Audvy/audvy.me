Brain–computer interfaces

Neuropoly

An EEG-powered interface that translates visual attention into moves on an online Monopoly board.

Turning visual attention into input

Our team built Neuropoly at the 2025 University at Buffalo Hackathon. Players look at one of several stimuli, each flashing at a different frequency, to choose a game action.

Four electrodes over the occipital lobe capture live EEG. The system estimates the dominant frequency in the signal, matches it to the selected stimulus, then sends the corresponding action to the online board.
